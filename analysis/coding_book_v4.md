## Scope and Data Sources

The analytical framework distinguishes between **two separate data sources**, each analysed with its own dimension:

| Data Source | Analytical Dimension | Coding Book |
|---|---|---|
| Presidential speeches, interviews, public discourses | **Dimension 1 — Discursive Delegitimization** | This document (v4) |
| Formal documentation (executive decrees, budget documents, reorganization records, official policy instruments) | **Dimension 2 — State Capability Erosion** | See separate D2 protocol |

**This codebook (v4) covers speech coding only.** Dimension 2 is documented separately and applied exclusively to formal documentation. Do not apply D2 codes to speeches, even when a speech references, justifies, or announces an institutional change — those passages are coded under Dimension 1 (they are rhetorical acts, not the institutional acts themselves).

> **Why the separation matters:** A speech announcing withdrawal from the 2030 Agenda is a discursive act — it delegitimizes multilateral governance and rhetorically frames an exit. The actual institutional act is the executive decree or official communication. Conflating the two would confuse the mechanism: D1 captures *how Milei legitimizes dismantling through rhetoric*; D2 captures *what is actually dismantled through formal action*. The article's dual mechanism is demonstrated by triangulating both data sources independently, then showing their co-articulation.

## The Abductive Approach

This is an **abductive (inductive-deductive)** framework: the seed categories below are deductive starting points derived from the theoretical literature. Emergent patterns from the data are captured in Phase 2 (Inductive). As of v4, one inductive category has been promoted to deductive status (`Selective_Empiricism`), one has been retired (`Reported_Denial`), and one has been eliminated (`Temporal_Appropriation`).

## Phase 1: Deductive (Seed Categories)

### Dimension 1 — Discursive Delegitimization of Sustainability Governance and Public Expertise

This dimension captures how presidential discourse **problematizes, delegitimizes, or reframes** the role of the state, sustainability governance, and scientific expertise. The key mechanism is **contested legitimacy**: Milei's rhetoric weakens the public and epistemic grounds for state action on climate, making that action politically and intellectually untenable — regardless of whether formal commitments remain in place.

Three sub-dimensions capture distinct rhetorical registers:

---

#### 1.1 — Delegitimization of State Intervention and Sustainability Governance

Passages where Milei delegitimizes the state as a legitimate actor in sustainability or environmental governance — not necessarily by denying climate change per se, but by denying the state's authority, effectiveness, or moral standing to act on it.

**Sub-codes:**

---

**`Market_Primacy`**  
Arguments asserting that free markets solve or manage environmental problems better than state coordination. The key move: any problem addressed by state climate policy is better addressed by markets — therefore the state has no legitimate role. Includes framing environmental outcomes as spontaneous results of market processes, property rights, or voluntary agreements.  
*Recognize by:* claims that capitalism eliminates poverty and environmental problems; invocation of Kirznerian market discovery; categorical denial that market failures exist ("market failure is an oxymoron"); the argument that the only context in which there can be harm is through non-voluntary transactions.

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “La conclusión es obvia: lejos de ser la causa de nuestros problemas, el capitalismo de libre empresa, como sistema económico, es la única herramienta que tenemos para terminar con el hambre, la pobreza y la indigencia, a lo largo y a lo ancho de todo el planeta. La evidencia empírica es incuestionable.” — The hockey-stick economic history culminates in the categorical Market_Primacy claim: free-enterprise capitalism is "the only tool" for ending poverty, and the conclusion is declared empirically self-evident.

2. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “The only thing such intervention achieves is the creation of new distortions within the price system, which in turn hinders economic calculation, savings and investment — ultimately leading to even more poverty in its wake or a mesh of abhorrent regulations, such as is the case in Europe, which kills economic growth.” — The denial of market failures restated verbatim from Davos 2024, now anchored to European climate regulation as the exemplary case of "abhorrent regulations that kill economic growth." The EU's climate regulatory architecture (ETS, CBAM, Fit for 55) is the world's most ambitious; naming it as evidence for the No_Market_Failures thesis directly targets the leading model of…

3. *(2025-03-01-apertura-sesiones-ordinarias-143.md)* “Además, eliminamos la obra pública —que era uno de los curros más grandes de la política—. Y aquí también quiero dejar en claro una de las grandes falacias y mentiras de la política para con la gente, cuando dicen esa mentira de que la obra pública genera trabajo. Le digo a los argentinos de bien que eso es falso.” — The sustained argument that public works are a net economic negative because they crowd out private spending is a classic market-primacy move with direct implications for climate governance. Climate transition policy relies substantially on public investment — green infrastructure, renewable energy buildout, adaptation works.

**`Anti_State_Anti_Bureaucratic`**  
Rhetoric attacking the state apparatus, bureaucracy, or public administration as inherently inefficient, predatory, parasitic, or coercive — applied to environmental or sustainability agencies. Distinct from `Anti_Casta` (which is a political-class critique) because this targets the *organizational form* of the state, not a specific political elite.  
*Recognize by:* metaphors of bureaucratic bloat ("hipertrofia del Estado"); images of comfortable bureaucrats living off others' labor; the intervention spiral argument (regulation creates distortions that justify more regulation); calls to "exterminate" the state apparatus.

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “Sin embargo, frente a la demostración teórica de que la intervención del estado es perjudicial, y la evidencia empírica de que fracasó, la solución que propondrán los colectivistas no es mayor libertad sino que es mayor regulación, generando una espiral descendiente de regulaciones hasta que todos seamos más pobres, y…” — "Un burócrata sentado en una oficina de lujo" personifies the regulatory state as a rent-seeking class who live off others' productivity. Sustainability agencies, CONICET researchers, and UNFCCC secretariat staff are implicitly "parasites" in this framing.

2. *(2025-09-24-milei-onu-80-asamblea-general.md)* “Así, los Estados contemporáneos han entrado en una dinámica donde el interés del político siempre será sacrificar el futuro en el altar del presente porque consumir el capital acumulado genera bienestar momentáneo y ese bienestar momentáneo genera votos.” — "Incendian el futuro para mantener caliente el presente" is the central rhetorical move of the entire speech: intergenerational responsibility language — the moral foundation of sustainability discourse — is co-opted and directed against the 2030 Agenda and the multilateral governance apparatus.

3. *(2026-03-01-apertura-sesiones-ordinarias-144.md)* “En el fondo, la única forma infalible de evitar los principales costos de la promiscuidad, el amiguismo y la corrupción en el Estado es reducirlo. El Estado presente, que pretende hacerse cargo de todos los órdenes de la vida, fracasó.” — The invocation of Ayn Rand's "ethics of emergency" to justify a minimal state, combined with the claim that any state "that pretends to take care of all dimensions of life" has failed, is a direct repudiation of the regulatory state's legitimacy in the domain of climate governance.

**`Anti_Casta`**  
Deployment of the "la casta" frame to delegitimize environmental institutions, climate ministries, sustainability technocrats, or global governance elites as part of the extractive political establishment. The mechanism: these actors do not serve the public but exploit governance positions for personal enrichment or ideological projects.  
*Recognize by:* explicit "la casta" language applied to governance actors; "casta privilegiada" applied to global governance elites; framing institutional actors as parasites ("viven de lo que otros producen") rather than public servants; the distinction between producers and extractors applied to state bureaucracies.

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “Lamentablemente en las últimas décadas, motivados por algunos deseos bien pensantes de querer ayudar al prójimo y otros por el deseo de querer pertenecer a una casta privilegiada, los principales líderes del mundo occidental han abandonado el modelo de la libertad, por distintas versiones, de lo que llamamos…” — "Pertenecer a una casta privilegiada" extends Milei's domestic anti-elite frame to the global governance level, targeting the WEF audience itself. International climate institutions and their leaders are pre-emptively delegitimized as members of a global caste pursuing privilege rather than planetary welfare — the same logic used domestically against environmental ministries…

2. *(2024-03-01-apertura-sesiones-ordinarias-142.md)* “Tras haber visto con mis propios ojos y en detalle la vulgaridad del despilfarro con el que la política se acostumbró a vivir, ratifico una vez más que la casta política es lo peor que le puede pasar a un país como el nuestro.” — Milei confirms and intensifies the "la casta" frame as the foundational political category of his governing project. This passage is the first explicit formalization of the casta indictment in a presidential address, establishing the frame that will subsequently be applied to environmental agencies, sustainability technocrats, and international governance bodies.

3. *(2026-03-01-apertura-sesiones-ordinarias-144.md)* “"Hay momentos en la historia en los que una Nación debe elegir su futuro. Nos han dicho que el Estado es nuestro protector, que el burócrata es nuestro salvador y que el político sabe más que el hombre libre; que debemos obedecer, que debemos depender. Pero la verdad es otra: el mundo solo tiene dos tipos de personas.” — The Huerta de Soto quote — introduced by the President and applied directly to the congressional opposition — delivers the parasites/producers binary in its most philosophically elaborated form: "Los que viven de lo que otros producen" (parasites, i.e., the political class) versus "los que producen todo lo que hace posible la vida moderna.

**`Delegitimization_of_Regulation`** *(v4: merged with former `Deregulation_Benefits`)*  
Arguments casting environmental regulation or public coordination mechanisms (carbon pricing, emissions standards, permits, monitoring requirements) as illegitimate interference in private activity or market dynamics, *or* arguments that removing such regulation produces positive economic and social outcomes. These are two faces of the same rhetorical move and are coded together.  
- *Negative face (delegitimization):* Goes beyond arguing regulations are costly — claims they are categorically illegitimate (coercive, theoretically unfounded, or structurally socialist). *Recognize by:* "market failure is an oxymoron"; framing regulation as coercion or a Trojan horse for state intrusion; the Hoppe non-aggression principle invoked against environmental standards; neoclassical externality theory framed as theoretically corrupt.  
- *Positive face (deregulation):* Narratives emphasizing the positive economic or social effects of removing environmental or sustainability-related regulations. *Recognize by:* explicit claims that deregulation generates growth and investment; the "don't pester innovators" argument; Europe as cautionary tale of regulatory overreach; "exterminar regulaciones" framing.  

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “El marco teórico al que me refiero es el de la teoría económica neoclásica, que diseña un instrumental que, sin quererlo, termina siendo funcional a la intromisión del estado.” — The attack on neoclassical economics demolishes the theoretical foundation of environmental regulation. Mainstream climate policy — carbon pricing, Pigouvian taxes, cap-and-trade — derives its justification from externality theory, precisely the "instrumental" Milei argues ends up serving state intrusion.

2. *(2024-03-01-apertura-sesiones-ordinarias-142.md)* “Por último, también firmamos un mega Decreto de Necesidad y Urgencia para, por primera vez en décadas, devolverles la libertad a los argentinos en vez de quitárselas con regulaciones sin sentido.” — The phrase "regulaciones sin sentido" does categorical ideological work: it asserts that regulations as a class are senseless, without needing to evaluate any specific regulation's rationale. This is not a technocratic critique but an a priori dismissal — the state's power to regulate is itself illegitimate.

3. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “The only thing such intervention achieves is the creation of new distortions within the price system, which in turn hinders economic calculation, savings and investment — ultimately leading to even more poverty in its wake or a mesh of abhorrent regulations, such as is the case in Europe, which kills economic growth.” — The positive deregulation argument paired with the Europe case: removing climate regulation restores economic calculation, investment, and growth. "Such as is the case in Europe, which kills economic growth" — Deregulation_Benefits applied to climate regulation specifically, with the EU's climate architecture as empirical evidence.
**Note:** When the passage clearly does both simultaneously (negative case + positive case), apply one code — do not stack two codes on the same passage.

**`Sustainability_as_Ideological_or_Harmful`**  
Framing sustainability governance, climate policy, or the green agenda as ideologically motivated, economically damaging, or socially harmful — regardless of whether climate change itself is acknowledged. Distinct from `Rejection_of_Multilateral_Governance` (which targets the governance *architecture*) and from `Anti_Socialism_Anti_Collectivism` (which targets the ideological *affiliation*) because this code applies when the policy *response* is framed as misguided or harmful independent of its institutional or ideological origins.  
*Recognize by:* "policies that attribute climate change to humans are false"; characterizing the 2030 Agenda as producing "malos resultados" for developing countries; framing the green agenda as anti-development or anti-human; "prejuicios ambientalistas absurdos"; Malthusian-anti-humanism accusation applied to environmentalism.

**Examples:**

1. *(2023-10-08-milei-segundo-debate-presidencial.md)* “A ver primero no mienta. Yo no niego el cambio climático. Lo que yo digo es que existe en la historia de la tierra un ciclo de temperaturas, es decir hay un comportamiento cíclico y este es el quinto punto del ciclo — donde la diferencia con los cuatro anteriores es que antes no estaba el ser humano.” — "Recaudar fondos para financiar vagos socialistas que escriben papers de cuarta" reframes climate policy as a rent-extraction mechanism for a parasitic academic class. The claim is not that climate policy is merely wrong — it is that climate policy exists as an institutional vehicle for redistributing resources to ideologically motivated, low-quality scholarship.

2. *(2024-09-24-milei-un-general-assembly.md)* “I want to be clear on the position of the Argentine agenda: the 2030 Agenda, although well-intentioned in its goals, is nothing more than a supranational government program, socialist in nature, which seeks to solve the problems of modernity with solutions that violate the sovereignty of nation states and violate…” — "An agenda that pretends to solve poverty with legislation that only deepens it" is Sustainability_as_Ideological_or_Harmful in its strongest formulation: not merely ineffective but actively counterproductive.

3. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “But once again, wokeism has managed to pervert this fundamental idea. From preserving the environment for human enjoyment, we have shifted to a fanatical environmentalism, where we humans are seen as a cancer that must be eradicated and economic development is considered little more than a crime against nature.” — "Economic development is considered a crime against nature" reframes climate regulation as anti-development ideology. Particularly effective for the Global South dimension: if climate governance criminalizes development, it condemns poor countries to permanent underdevelopment in the name of ideology.

**`Rejection_of_Multilateral_Governance`** *(v4: definition broadened beyond environmental governance)*  
Rejection of multilateral governance institutions, international sustainability agreements, or supranational bodies as illegitimate impositions on national sovereignty, expressions of globalist ideology, or instruments of ideological coercion. Applied when Milei *rhetorically frames* multilateral governance as illegitimate — regardless of whether a formal withdrawal has occurred. The definition in v4 is broadened: the rejection need not be explicitly environmental; when Milei attacks multilateral institutions on sovereignty or ideological grounds, the climate obstruction function is established by the broader corpus context.  
*Recognize by:* declarations that the 2030 Agenda is "supranational government, socialist in nature"; calls to "drain" supranational organizations; invitations for a counter-coalition ("la agenda de la libertad"); characterizing multilateral bodies as "ideologues of barbarity"; the four-principle UN reform blueprint; "La era de la cooperación global sin brújula moral ha terminado."

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “Lamentablemente, estas ideas nocivas han impregnado fuertemente en nuestra sociedad. Los neo-marxistas han sabido cooptar el sentido común de occidente. Lograron esto gracias a la apropiación de los medios de comunicación, de la cultura, de las universidades, y sí, también de los organismos internacionales.” — "Los organismos internacionales" as the final node of the neo-Marxist cooptation network places the UNFCCC process, the 2030 Agenda framework, and multilateral climate funds within a conspiracy of ideological capture.

2. *(2024-09-24-milei-un-general-assembly.md)* “I want to be clear on the position of the Argentine agenda: the 2030 Agenda, although well-intentioned in its goals, is nothing more than a supranational government program, socialist in nature, which seeks to solve the problems of modernity with solutions that violate the sovereignty of nation states and violate…” — The 2030 Agenda — the overarching UN sustainability framework encompassing SDGs, climate action (SDG 13), clean energy, and biodiversity — is formally declared "a supranational government program, socialist in nature." Delivered before the General Assembly, this is the highest-profile Rejection_of_Multilateral_Governance in the corpus.

3. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “Because I must say that forums like this one have been protagonists and promoters of the sinister agenda of wokeism that is doing so much damage to the Western world.” — WEF is named a "protagonist and promoter" of a "sinister agenda" — the institution that convenes global climate governance (the Davos Consensus, net-zero pledges, green capitalism frameworks) is delegitimized as ideologically captured before any specific climate argument is made.

#### 1.2 — Delegitimization of Public Science and Expertise

Passages where Milei delegitimizes public scientific institutions, academic knowledge, or expert authority — undermining the epistemic infrastructure that justifies sustainability policy.

**Sub-codes:**

---

**`Attack_on_Universities_and_Research_Institutions`** *(v4: merged from `Attack_on_Universities` + `Attack_on_Public_Research_Institutions`)*  
Criticism of public universities or public research bodies (e.g., CONICET) as centers of ideological indoctrination, wasteful rent-seeking, or epistemically corrupt knowledge production. These institutions form the expert community that underpins regulatory intervention and collective climate governance; delegitimizing them erodes the knowledge base for climate action.  
*Recognize by:* framing universities as "indoctrination centers" or producers of ideologically captured knowledge; characterizing public researchers as rent-seekers funded by taxpayers; naming CONICET or equivalent bodies as wasteful; "university expansion as political clientelism" argument.

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-03-01-apertura-sesiones-ordinarias-142.md)* “En el plano de la Educación Superior, la creación de universidades se ha convertido en un negocio más de la política y en los profesorados e institutos de formación inicial de docentes se imparte, cada vez más, adoctrinamiento político, en lugar de educación.” — Milei frames teacher-training colleges and universities as sites of "political indoctrination" rather than education, attacking the epistemic infrastructure that produces Argentina's scientific and professional classes.

2. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “This is the great epidemic of our time that must be cured. This is the cancer we need to get rid of. This ideology has colonized the world's most important institutions — from the political parties and governments of leading Western nations to global governance organizations, even NGOs, universities and media outlets.” — Universities appear in the list of colonized institutions alongside global governance organizations and media. Universities are the primary sites where climate scientists are trained, climate research is produced, and the expert community underpinning IPCC assessment reports is formed.

3. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “And wokeism also seeks to hijack our future because by dominating the faculties of the world's most prestigious universities, it is shaping the elites of our countries to reject and deny the culture, ideas and values that made us great.” — The university attack reappears in a futurity register: universities are not just producing bad knowledge now but "shaping the elites" who will govern and make policy in the future. **`Attack_on_Experts_and_Technocrats`** *(v4: absorbs former `Rejection_of_Expert_Mediation`)*  
Delegitimization of expert authority — scientists, economists, policy specialists, international technocrats — as a political class that imposes knowledge on society rather than serving it. Includes both attacks on specific experts and arguments that expert bodies should not mediate or influence policymaking. The "attack" (discrediting the person/institution) and the "mediation rejection" (delegitimizing the governance role) are analytically unified.  
*Recognize by:* "vagos socialistas que escriben papers de cuarta"; "amparados en un marco teórico equivocado"; framing international organizations' experts as "international bureaucrats who impose a lifestyle"; arguments that decisions should be left to markets and individuals rather than technocratic consensus; framing all epistemic institutions as nodes in an ideological network that should be bypassed.

**Examples:**

1. *(2023-10-08-milei-segundo-debate-presidencial.md)* “A ver primero no mienta. Yo no niego el cambio climático. Lo que yo digo es que existe en la historia de la tierra un ciclo de temperaturas, es decir hay un comportamiento cíclico y este es el quinto punto del ciclo — donde la diferencia con los cuatro anteriores es que antes no estaba el ser humano.” — "Vagos socialistas que escriben papers de cuarta" is the rawest expert delegitimation in the corpus — climate scientists reduced to lazy, ideologically motivated rent-seekers producing worthless work.

2. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “El colectivismo - al inhibir estos procesos de descubrimiento y al dificultar la apropiación de lo descubierto - ata al emprendedor de las manos y le imposibilita producir mejores bienes y ofrecer mejores servicios a un mejor precio.” — The entire authority apparatus — academic, international, political, theoretical — is presented as a unified bloc that "demonizes" capitalism. For sustainability governance: IPCC, UNEP, national science ministries, and environmental economics are all expressions of the same anti-capitalist ideological formation.

3. *(2024-09-24-milei-un-general-assembly.md)* “It has been precisely the adoption of this agenda, which obeys privileged interests, the abandonment of the principles — outlined in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights — that has distorted the role of this institution.” — Framing the UN's COVID-19 pandemic response as "a crime against humanity" attacks both the institution and the expert consensus (epidemiological, public health) that underpinned the lockdown policies.

**`Climate_Skepticism_or_Dismissal`**  
Explicit or implicit dismissal of climate science, environmental science, or the scientific basis for sustainability governance. Includes direct denial, natural cycles arguments, relativization of attribution findings, or claims that the scientific evidence supports the skeptic position.  
*Recognize by:* the five-temperature-cycles argument; "all policies that blame humans for climate change are false"; "science and data are on our side" when deployed against climate consensus; the "I don't deny climate change, but..." structure followed by denial-equivalent claims.

**Examples:**

1. *(2023-10-08-milei-segundo-debate-presidencial.md)* “A ver primero no mienta. Yo no niego el cambio climático. Lo que yo digo es que existe en la historia de la tierra un ciclo de temperaturas, es decir hay un comportamiento cíclico y este es el quinto punto del ciclo — donde la diferencia con los cuatro anteriores es que antes no estaba el ser humano.” — Milei performs the disclaimer-then-dismiss move: "I don't deny climate change" is immediately followed by the natural-cycles attribution, which reproduces effective denial while maintaining rhetorical deniability.

2. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “And yet, when we argue that the Earth has already gone through five abrupt temperature change cycles, and that during four of those men was not even around, we are called 'Flat Earthers' in order to discredit our ideas, even though science and the data are on our side.” — The five-cycles argument — Earth has undergone abrupt temperature changes without human presence — is the natural cycles denialist claim deployed as a counter to anthropogenic attribution. This is the clearest direct climate science denial in the corpus.

3. *(Illustrative — constructed, not drawn from corpus)* “Los glaciares patagónicos siempre avanzaron y retrocedieron a lo largo de la historia; atribuirlo exclusivamente a la actividad humana es, cuanto menos, una simplificación interesada.” — Shows the pattern applied to a specific climate impact (glacial retreat) rather than temperature cycles: the impact is attributed to natural variability, and anthropogenic attribution is dismissed as "interesada" (self-interested) rather than engaged on the science. Included as a template for cases not yet in the corpus.

**`Pseudo_Science_or_Ideological_Science`**  
Accusations that climate science, environmental research, or sustainability studies constitute pseudoscience, ideologically motivated inquiry, or politically captured knowledge production. The move: not that specific findings are wrong, but that the entire institutional production of climate knowledge is epistemically corrupt — captured by neo-Marxist ideology, political agendas, or self-interested academic culture.  
*Recognize by:* "amparados en un marco teórico equivocado" applied structurally to the scientific community; neo-Marxist cooptation of universities and international organizations producing ideological rather than reliable knowledge; the "Flat Earthers" inversion (those who accept climate science are the deniers of reality).  

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “El problema esencial de occidente hoy es que no sólo debemos enfrentarnos a quienes siguen bregando por el socialismo empobrecedor; sino también a nuestros propios líderes, pensadores y académicos que, amparados en un marco teórico equivocado, socavan los fundamentos del sistema que nos ha dado la mayor expansión de…” — "Amparados en un marco teórico equivocado" frames the entire academic and expert community as epistemically compromised: their framework is wrong, and therefore their outputs — including climate science, environmental economics, and sustainability research — are systematically distorted.

2. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “And yet, when we argue that the Earth has already gone through five abrupt temperature change cycles, and that during four of those men was not even around, we are called 'Flat Earthers' in order to discredit our ideas, even though science and the data are on our side.” — "Science and the data are on our side" directly appropriates scientific authority for the denialist position. The "Flat Earthers" label is framed as a delegitimation tactic rather than a substantive critique. The epistemic authority reversal: consensus scientists become dogmatists silencing dissent, while the climate skeptic becomes the true empiricist.

3. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “Lamentablemente, estas ideas nocivas han impregnado fuertemente en nuestra sociedad. Los neo-marxistas han sabido cooptar el sentido común de occidente. Lograron esto gracias a la apropiación de los medios de comunicación, de la cultura, de las universidades, y sí, también de los organismos internacionales.” — The "cooptation" narrative positions universities and international organizations as ideologically captured producers of knowledge in service of a political agenda. IPCC, UNEP, WMO, and the media disseminating climate research are all nodes in an ideological network — climate science is not wrong because its methods are bad but because its institutions have been colonized by…
**Note:** `Pseudo_Science` operates at the *institutional* level (the whole knowledge enterprise is corrupt); `Climate_Skepticism_or_Dismissal` operates at the *claims* level (specific findings are wrong). A passage can carry both only if it genuinely makes both moves — apply the overcoding rule before stacking.

**`Selective_Empiricism`** *(v4: promoted from inductive to deductive)*  
A rhetorical pattern in which Milei deploys the language of empirical authority exclusively for pro-market claims while simultaneously characterizing opposing institutional knowledge (including climate science) as ideologically captured and invalid. The diagnostic feature is the *dual move*: appropriating empirical authority for one's own position while denying the same epistemic standing to climate science. Distinct from `Pseudo_Science_or_Ideological_Science` (which labels science as ideological) because here Milei *claims the empirical ground* for his own claims rather than simply dismissing the opposition.  
*Recognize by:* "La evidencia empírica es incuestionable" (about capitalism's poverty record) followed by dismissal of institutional science; invoking "science and data" on one side while characterizing IPCC or sustainability research as political; asymmetric treatment of evidence — rigorous empiricism for market claims, blanket dismissal for climate claims.

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “La conclusión es obvia: lejos de ser la causa de nuestros problemas, el capitalismo de libre empresa, como sistema económico, es la única herramienta que tenemos para terminar con el hambre, la pobreza y la indigencia, a lo largo y a lo ancho de todo el planeta. La evidencia empírica es incuestionable.” — "La evidencia empírica es incuestionable" is the canonical Selective_Empiricism instance in the corpus: Milei claims unquestionable empirical authority for the pro-market historical narrative, but later in the same speech systematically dismisses opposing academic and institutional knowledge (including climate science) as ideologically captured.

2. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “And yet, when we argue that the Earth has already gone through five abrupt temperature change cycles, and that during four of those men was not even around, we are called 'Flat Earthers' in order to discredit our ideas, even though science and the data are on our side.” — Clearest corpus instance of Selective_Empiricism: "science and data are on our side" legitimates the natural-cycles claim while framing the institutional scientific community's response as ideological silencing. The dual move — appropriating empiricism for the denialist side while denying it to the consensus side — is the defining feature of this pattern.

3. *(Illustrative — constructed, not drawn from corpus)* “Los números de pobreza y crecimiento no mienten, ahí está la evidencia. En cambio, los modelos climáticos son proyecciones ideológicas disfrazadas de ciencia.” — Shows the diagnostic dual move with a different empirical anchor: statistical authority is claimed without qualification for the economic claim ("los números no mienten"), while climate modeling is simultaneously waved away as "ideological projections" rather than engaged as data. Included as a template for cases not yet in the corpus.

#### 1.3 — Cultural Backlash

Passages where sustainability, environmentalism, or climate governance is embedded within a broader culture-war framing — linked to socialism, gender ideology, or civilizational threat. The mechanism is **ideological bundling**: associating climate policy with a broader political enemy, removing it from its own empirical domain and relocating it within a unified ideological target.

**Sub-codes:**

---

**`Woke_Bundling`**  
Merging climate, gender, DEI, or sustainability into a single "woke" or progressive enemy. The rhetorical move treats these as co-expressions of the same collectivist-progressive project, allowing the emotional and moral charge of one issue to contaminate the others.  
*Recognize by:* enumerating environmentalism alongside feminism, gender ideology, or immigration as "heads of the same beast"; "Wokism also manifests in... radical environmentalism"; the Malthus-abortion-climate chain; "ridiculous policies like gender ideology or radical environmentalism" bundled in the same phrase.

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “Otro de los conflictos que los socialistas plantean es el del hombre contra la naturaleza. Sostienen que los seres humanos dañamos el planeta y que debe ser protegido a toda costa, incluso llegando a abogar por mecanismos de control poblacional o en la agenda sangrienta del aborto.” — Feminism then environmentalism, both introduced as socialist "new battles" — the formal Woke_Bundling move. The emotional charge of the "ridiculous and unnatural" gender conflict transfers directly to climate via juxtaposition.

2. *(2024-09-24-milei-un-general-assembly.md)* “Nor has the tutelage of the World Economic Forum helped, where ridiculous policies are promoted with Malthusian blinders on — such as "Zero Emission" policies — which harm poor countries in particular.” — "Zero Emission" policies are listed in the same breath as "policies linked to sexual and reproductive rights" — climate policy bundled with population/reproductive policy via the Malthusian linkage.

3. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “But once again, wokeism has managed to pervert this fundamental idea. From preserving the environment for human enjoyment, we have shifted to a fanatical environmentalism, where we humans are seen as a cancer that must be eradicated and economic development is considered little more than a crime against nature.” — The pivot from feminism to environmentalism using "Wokism also manifests in..." is the formal Woke_Bundling move: climate governance is positioned as another chapter in the same ideological story, governed by the same analytical template applied to feminism.

---

**`Anti_Socialism_Anti_Collectivism`** *(v4: renamed from `Neo_Marxism`)*  
Framing environmentalism or the green agenda explicitly as disguised socialism, Marxism, or collectivism — "watermelon politics" (green outside, red inside). The claim: climate governance is not a genuine environmental response but a strategic vehicle for socialist state expansion. The v4 label (`Anti_Socialism_Anti_Collectivism`) describes the rhetorical function analytically, rather than mirroring Milei's own polemical vocabulary ("neo-marxismo"). The scope is broadened to capture anti-collectivism framings that do not explicitly invoke Marxism.  
*Recognize by:* "the conflict socialists propose is man against nature"; environmental movement as "new agenda" of neo-Marxists after failing with class struggle; Malthusian premise as disguised population-control socialism; any passage framing the green agenda as a vehicle for collective control over individual or market activity.

**Examples:**

1. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “These are all various heads of the same beast aimed at justifying the state's expansion through the appropriation and distortion of noble causes.” — Environmentalism is listed (alongside feminism, gender ideology, and immigration) as one of several "heads of the same beast aimed at justifying the state's expansion." Neo_Marxism's core formulation: these causes are strategic tools for state expansion, not genuine moral concerns.

2. *(2026-03-01-apertura-sesiones-ordinarias-144.md)* “La verdadera batalla de nuestro tiempo es cultural, filosófica y moral. Es elegir el sistema que sacó de la pobreza a millones. Es para dejar de ser una Nación inmadura, que dilapida el futuro para repartir beneficios en el presente; y convertirnos en una nación madura, que planifica las reglas de juego para que haya…” — The framing of "la verdadera batalla de nuestro tiempo" as cultural, philosophical, and moral — a battle between the "system that lifted millions from poverty" (capitalism/market freedom) and an unnamed adversary — is the neo-Marxism code at its most developed.

3. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “But once again, wokeism has managed to pervert this fundamental idea. From preserving the environment for human enjoyment, we have shifted to a fanatical environmentalism, where we humans are seen as a cancer that must be eradicated and economic development is considered little more than a crime against nature.” — "Economic development is considered little more than a crime against nature" frames climate regulation as anti-civilization and anti-progress ideology. The "cancer" metaphor is analytically notable: Milei applies "cancer" both to woke ideology (which must be cured) and to how environmentalists supposedly view humanity — a double inversion equating climate advocates with those…

---

**`Civilizational_Defense`**  
A master framing that positions Western civilization as under existential threat from socialism or collectivism, with climate governance as one front in that civilizational war. Operates at a higher level of abstraction than `Anti_Socialism_Anti_Collectivism` (which labels ideological content) — here the stakes are the survival and moral integrity of Western civilization itself.  
*Recognize by:* "Occidente está en peligro"; the multi-year Davos arc as civilizational recovery narrative; America-as-beacon framing; Trump-Milei alignment as civilizational vanguard; "moral occidental como política de Estado" permanence claims; climate governance as one battlefront in a broader civilizational conflict rather than a discrete policy domain.

**Examples:**

1. *(2024-01-17-milei-wef-davos.md)* “Lamentablemente en las últimas décadas, motivados por algunos deseos bien pensantes de querer ayudar al prójimo y otros por el deseo de querer pertenecer a una casta privilegiada, los principales líderes del mundo occidental han abandonado el modelo de la libertad, por distintas versiones, de lo que llamamos…” — The opening sentences establish the master civilizational frame for the entire speech: Western civilization is under existential threat from socialism, and the WEF audience itself is identified as part of a "casta privilegiada" complicit in that decline. This frame pre-loads every subsequent critique — including of environmental policy — with civilizational stakes.

2. *(2024-09-24-milei-un-general-assembly.md)* “I want to be clear on the position of the Argentine agenda: the 2030 Agenda, although well-intentioned in its goals, is nothing more than a supranational government program, socialist in nature, which seeks to solve the problems of modernity with solutions that violate the sovereignty of nation states and violate…” — The UN is positioned as an agent of collectivist civilizational decline. Argentina's dissent is framed as a defense of the principles — life, liberty, property — that define legitimate civilization. The speech functions as a civilizational declaration: Argentina aligning itself in a conflict between freedom and collectivism, with the 2030 Agenda as a key battlefield.

3. *(2025-01-23-milei-davos-speech.md)* “And this organization, along with the most influential supranational bodies, have been the ideologues of this barbarity. Multilateral lending organizations have been instruments of extortion, while many national governments, and especially the European Union, have acted as their armed wing.” — "Worship of a sinister and murderous ideology, as if it were a golden calf" elevates the stakes to civilizational-religious dimensions. The WEF-led governance consensus — including its climate architecture — is not an error but an idolatry that must be dismantled to save Western civilization. Civilizational_Defense at maximum intensity.

**Boundary between `Anti_Socialism_Anti_Collectivism` and `Civilizational_Defense`:**  
Use `Civilizational_Defense` when the stakes are explicitly *existential* for Western civilization as a whole. Use `Anti_Socialism_Anti_Collectivism` when the passage frames a specific policy, movement, or agenda as socialist or collectivist. When a passage does both, apply the overcoding rule and choose the dominant register.

**Definition (retained for reference):** A rhetorical strategy in which Milei co-opts the normative language of intergenerational responsibility — the moral foundation of sustainability discourse — and redeploys it to argue *against* state intervention and multilateral climate governance.

**Status:** Eliminated from active use in v4 following peer review. **If the pattern re-emerges:** Apply only when *both* conditions are met: (1) Milei uses vocabulary recognizably from sustainability discourse (intergenerational responsibility, futurity, "protecting future generations"); (2) he redirects that vocabulary explicitly *against* a sustainability governance mechanism (not just against the state in general). If both conditions are met, apply as `[CODE: INDUCTIVE > Temporal_Appropriation]` and flag for team review before formalizing.

## Instructions for the Analyst Agent (v4)

1. **Read this codebook in full** before touching any speech files.

2. **Apply only Dimension 1 codes** (D1.1, D1.2, D1.3) to speech files. Do not apply any D2 code to a speech, even if the speech announces or justifies an institutional action.

2b. **Scope rule — general anti-state/anti-casta/anti-regulation rhetoric does not require an explicit climate mention (added 2026-07-19, resolving a documented inconsistency; see `analysis/_draft_v4_batch2_consistency_audit.md`).** The master corpus database's `clima` column (✔ / ~ / blank) is a *speech-selection* heuristic for deciding which speeches to code next — it is not a passage-level filter. Once a speech has been selected for coding, do not withhold a D1.1/D1.2/D1.3 code from a passage merely because that specific passage does not name climate, the environment, or the 2030 Agenda. The D1.1 framing above is explicit that Milei's rhetoric "weakens the public and epistemic grounds for state action on climate... regardless of whether formal commitments remain in place" — general `Anti_Casta`, `Anti_State_Anti_Bureaucratic`, `Delegitimization_of_Regulation`, `Market_Primacy`, `Attack_on_Experts_and_Technocrats`, `Attack_on_Universities_and_Research_Institutions`, and `Anti_Socialism_Anti_Collectivism` moves are codeable wherever they occur, because they constitute the discursive infrastructure that later gets applied to climate governance specifically — the same theoretical logic already used for the `Anti_Casta` example in `2024-03-01-apertura-sesiones-ordinarias-142.md` ("la casta política es lo peor que le puede pasar a un país como el nuestro" — no climate content, coded anyway) and for the `Delegitimization_of_Regulation` example in the same speech ("regulaciones sin sentido" — general economic deregulation, coded anyway).

If the answer to the first question is yes, code it under the matching D1 sub-code even with zero climate vocabulary.
